About Earthquakes

Understanding the science behind seismic events

What Causes Earthquakes?

Earthquakes are caused by the sudden release of energy in the Earth's crust, which creates seismic waves. The Earth's crust is divided into several tectonic plates that constantly move. When these plates collide, slide past each other, or pull apart, they cause earthquakes.

Types of Earthquakes

Tectonic Earthquakes

The most common type, caused by the movement of tectonic plates along fault lines.

Volcanic Earthquakes

Occur during volcanic eruptions when magma and rock move beneath the Earth's surface.

Induced Earthquakes

Caused by human activities such as mining, reservoir construction, and geothermal energy production.

The Richter Scale

The Richter magnitude scale measures the energy released by an earthquake. It ranges from 1 (barely noticeable) to 9+ (devastating). Each whole number increase represents roughly 30 times more energy release.

  • 1-3: Minor earthquakes, not usually felt
  • 4-5: Moderate earthquakes, noticeable shaking
  • 6-7: Strong earthquakes, can cause damage
  • 8+: Major earthquakes, severe damage likely
