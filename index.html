What Are Earthquakes?

Earthquakes are sudden, rapid shaking of the Earth caused by the release of energy in the Earth's crust. They can occur anywhere on Earth and at any time. Most earthquakes occur along the edges of tectonic plates, where massive rock slabs that make up the Earth's crust and upper mantle collide and slide past each other.

Understanding earthquakes helps us build better structures, prepare communities, and save lives.
